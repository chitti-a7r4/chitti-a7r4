### About me

I’m Shiva, or Chitti. I study electronics and communications engineering at NEHU in Shillong and run Jala Venkat Technologies. I built **[Clash Ledger](https://github.com/chitti-a7r4/coc-tracker)**, an open-source Android upgrade tracker and home screen widget for Clash of Clans that reached 47+ stars and received an official appreciation reply from the **Clash of Clans official team** on Reddit.

I built My NEHU to centralize campus notices, academic updates, and emergency resources. For BarOps, I worked on a different problem: letting bartenders in Thailand record stock changes even when the connection drops. With Kisan LoRa, I'm engineering long-range (up to 8 km) wireless agricultural pump controllers. During my internship at IIT Guwahati, I studied how individual bit flips affect memory wear. I’m also working on Bharat E-Wallet, a way for students to pay without carrying a phone.
